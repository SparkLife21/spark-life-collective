The Spark Life Framework

7 Aspect Groups.
A More God-Aligned You.

Our framework helps people grow in every area of life, because true impact starts from the inside out. Instead of working on one corner of your life at a time, it lays the whole of it out together, so you can see where God may be inviting you further in.

Set your soul on fire for Christ

7Aspect Groups
21Aspects of Life
2–3Aspects Per Retreat

Stability

  • Wealth
  • Accountability
  • Health

The ground you stand on: what you steward, who can tell you the truth about it, and the body you carry it all in.

Connection

  • Energy
  • Intimacy
  • Relationships

Who gets the real you. How closeness is built, and what it costs when there is nothing left to give.

Empowerment

  • Career
  • Purpose
  • Individuality

The work in front of you and why it matters. Doing it as the person God actually made, not a copy of someone else.

Devotion

  • Adversity
  • Adaptability
  • Comfort

What holds when circumstances do not. Staying faithful through hardship and change, and knowing where you go for rest.

Expression

  • Creativity
  • Communication
  • Appearance

What comes out of you. Making things, saying things plainly, and how you show up in a room.

Exploration

  • Adventure
  • Play
  • Dreaming

Room to wonder. Adventure, unhurried play, and permission to imagine what could be.

Belief

  • Influence
  • Curiosity
  • Spirituality

What you are anchored to, and what that anchor does to the people around you.

How It Works

From the whole picture to a next step.

Step One
See the whole picture

Seven groups, twenty-one aspects, laid out together instead of scattered and unexamined.

Step Two
Notice the invitation

Some aspects are steady. Others are the ones you avoid. That is usually where God is asking for attention.

Step Three
Join a retreat built on them

Each retreat gathers around specific aspects. Root & Rise takes up Spirituality and Adversity over two days, live.

Step Four
Keep going together

Growth holds when someone else knows about it. Retreats are conversation and prayer, not a lecture you watch alone.
